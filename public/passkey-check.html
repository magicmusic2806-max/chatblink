Passkey check

This page makes the same WebAuthn call "Add this device" makes, then loosens one setting at a time until this device accepts one. It never contacts the server, so nothing is added to your account — the challenges here are generated in the page. If a step offers a picker for another device, cancel it: the next step runs by itself.

A step that succeeds leaves a test passkey on this device, named passkey-check. Delete it from your password manager afterwards.
